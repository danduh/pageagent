07 · Open decisions for the human owner

Surfaced, not silently resolved. D1–D7 were raised by the brief's own analysis; D8–D14 are owner-level policy calls where the brief takes a working position pending sign-off.

Surfaced by this brief
D1 · Side-panel widthOpen

Pressure-test the 320–400px column against a 50–100-Tool page and the truthful gate preview before locking. The one trigger to revisit the form factor.

D2 · Non-sighted gate anchorOpen

What is the verifiable equivalent of "locate-on-page highlight" for a blind SR user, given it's a load-bearing confidence signal?

D3 · Color-blind validationOpen

Halide-brand vs Verdant-safe, Offshore vs both, under protanopia / deuteranopia and greyscale. Tune to AA (AAA body where reachable), both themes.

D4 · Hyperlegible: default or opt-inOpen

Flagship-persona benefit of default-on vs the inlined side-panel font payload. (Toggle is wired live above.)

D5 · Default summon keybindingOpen

Open-and-focus capability is settled; the specific default binding is not.

D6 · Mixed-turn locus semanticsOpen

Compound (reasoning-locus + audio-egress marker) vs most-exposed-path + "what left" label. Either must suppress the resting affordance while audio leaves.

D7 · Tier-0 misclassification riskOpen

Confidence bar + conservative bias + one-tap reverse mitigate a confidently-wrong reversibility call but don't eliminate it. Owner sets the acceptable posture and threshold.

Owner policy · working position noted, ratification required
D8 · Consent & auto-executionRatify

WP: Tier-0 executes without a gate; scan-time micro-disclosure covers "what I read here"; per-site trust (LATER) is the durable consent surface.

D9 · What is "destructive"Ratify

WP: the reversibility ladder plus the reversibility-confidence bar decide, biased conservative on high-stakes origins.

D10 · Hard-stop listRatify

WP: Tier-2 class — pay/send money, delete account, sign out, irreversible send — can never be silenced by any trust setting. A configurable floor is not a floor.

D11 · Low-confidence scanRatify

WP: list tools honestly with confidence signals, but decline to act on ambiguous destructive candidates and route to Execute.

D12 · Sites resisting automationUnresolved

Proceed as user-agent, warn, or refrain? Per-site configurable? Not resolved by this brief — shapes onboarding and per-site copy.

D13 · Which persona winsConfirm

WP: the accessibility user leads and governs conflicts (target sizes, motion, live-region altitude). Confirm the ordering.

D14 · Form-factor & IARatify

WP: Chrome side-panel home + five-surface IA as the committed build target. The width caveat (D1) is the one dimension left open.
